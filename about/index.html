    About Magic House

    Handmade creations for a more beautiful world.

    The beauty is in the details

    Magic House is a Lebanese brand of handmade decorative pieces. We cast and finish gypsum by hand to make incense burners and boxes, candles and candle holders, decorative trays, coasters, souvenirs, and gift sets.

    Because every piece is made by hand, no two are exactly alike. Many designs can be made in the colors you choose, and we put together sets for weddings, baptisms, baby showers, Mother’s Day, holidays, and corporate gifts.

    Ordering is simple: pick a piece, tap Order via WhatsApp, and we confirm availability and delivery with you directly.

    Questions, Answered

    What people usually ask before ordering.

    What products does Magic House offer?

    Magic House makes handmade gypsum decor: incense burners and incense boxes, candles and candle holders, decorative trays, souvenirs and gift sets, and home accessories.

    Are Magic House products handmade?

    Yes. Every piece is cast and finished by hand in Lebanon, so small variations between pieces are part of their character.

    Can I request customized gifts?

    Yes. Many pieces can be made in custom colors, and gift sets can be put together for occasions. Send your idea on WhatsApp and we will tell you what is possible.

    How can I order from Magic House?

    Tap Order via WhatsApp on any product. A message with the product name and link opens in WhatsApp, and we confirm availability, price, and delivery with you there.

    Does Magic House offer products for special occasions?

    Yes. Magic House pieces are made for weddings, baptisms, baby showers, Mother's Day, holidays, and corporate souvenirs, as well as everyday decor.
