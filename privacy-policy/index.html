    Privacy Policy

    Last updated October 26, 2026

    This website is a catalog. You cannot pay on it, and it does not create accounts.

    Information you share with us

    When you order through WhatsApp or send the contact form, you share your name, phone number, optional email address, and message. We use this only to answer you, prepare your order, and arrange delivery.

    WhatsApp

    Order buttons open WhatsApp, which is run by Meta. Messages you send there are covered by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

    Cookies and analytics

    This website does not set advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

    Sharing

    We do not sell your information. We share it only with a delivery partner when needed to deliver your order.

    Your choices

    You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the information you have sent us. Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram (@magicchouse.lb).
